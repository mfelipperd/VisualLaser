## 2024-05-17 - Missing sizes property for fill images
**Learning:** Found an anti-pattern in the codebase where Next.js `<Image>` components with the `fill` property are missing the required `sizes` attribute. This causes Next.js to serve unnecessarily large images to users on smaller screens (e.g. mobile devices), negatively affecting performance and bandwidth. Specifically, found in `components/ConveniosHighlight.tsx`, `components/equipe/DoctorsCatalog.tsx`, and `components/landing-page/LandingHero.tsx`.
**Action:** When working on `<Image fill>` components, always ensure the `sizes` attribute is provided based on the layout rules and device usage, for example `sizes="(max-width: 768px) 100vw, (max-width: 1200px) 50vw, 33vw"` or something similar based on the context.
